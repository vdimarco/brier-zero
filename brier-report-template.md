## Filling rules (internal — never ships)

1. **The verdict sentence is the whole report.** Write it last, from the
   numbers. If you can't write it without a hedge, the sample is too small — say
   *that* as the verdict.
2. **Never round in the flattering direction.** Four decimals on Brier, one on
   percentages, always.
3. **A bad result is publishable and a good one is suspicious.** If an agent
   comes out looking great, recheck the sample before shipping.
4. **Attack your own method in the Method section**, above the fold, not in a
   footer. Readers who find the caveat themselves discount everything; readers
   handed it trust the rest.
5. **One CTA, at the bottom, after the value.** No mid-report interruption.
6. **Never publish a named customer's agent without written permission.** Public
   reports cover publicly available models only.
