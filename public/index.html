Who lifts the trophy?

Matches

Group-stage probabilities are for the 90-minute result (extra time and penalties count as a draw). Knockout probabilities are simply who advances — no draw. Each card shows the consensus of all models, with team-by-team numbers above the bar.

Model leaderboard

Brier score over each match's outcomes: 0 is a perfect forecast, a know-nothing coin flip scores 0.667 on a three-way group match and 0.5 on a two-way knockout tie, 2 is maximally wrong. Lower is better. Click a model for its performance over time.
